META ADS • LEAD GENERATION

Turning product awareness into customer conversations.

I worked on digital marketing campaigns for Infosys Imagic, promoting weighing equipment and solutions to potential customers through Facebook advertising.

The campaign combined advertising creative, persuasive messaging and a direct WhatsApp enquiry journey to make it easier for interested prospects to ask questions, request pricing and learn more about the products.
